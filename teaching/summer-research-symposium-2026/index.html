Summer Group Research

Summer 2026 research symposium schedule and paper discussion plan.

Meeting Information

  • Meeting Time: Tuesdays, 10:30-11:30 AM (flex up to 12:00 if needed)
  • Room: SCEN 204
  • Format:
    • Main Presentation: 20 min + 10 min Q&A
    • Paper Review: 20 min

Goal

Practice independent research and engage with foundational and recent work in generative models through presentation, discussion, and critical analysis.

Schedule

May 19

  • Irina Afanasyeva (Graduate) — Main Presentation (20 + 10 Q&A)
  • Bashar Zaqebeh (Graduate) — Paper Review (20 min): Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (2013), Kingma and Welling
  • Daniel Kwame Okyere (Graduate) — Paper Review (20 min): Stochastic Backpropagation and Approximate Inference in Deep Generative Models (2014), Rezende, Mohamed, and Wierstra

May 26

  • Sebastian Wilcox (Undergraduate) — Main Presentation (20 + 10 Q&A)
  • Innocent Obed Awidi (Graduate) — Paper Review (20 min): Importance Weighted Autoencoders (2015), Burda, Grosse, and Salakhutdinov
  • Irina Afanasyeva (Graduate) — Paper Review (20 min): Generative Adversarial Nets (2014), Goodfellow et al.

June 2

  • Anjuman Ara Rashid (Graduate) — Main Presentation (20 + 10 Q&A)
  • Soumya Chauhan (Graduate) — Main Presentation (20 + 10 Q&A)
  • Sebastian Wilcox (Undergraduate) — Paper Review (20 min): Wasserstein GAN (WGAN) (2017), Arjovsky, Chintala, and Bottou

July 2 (Thursday)
